I am a PIMS Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at Simon Fraser University, where I work with Weiran Sun. Before that I completed my PhD at ETH Zürich under the supervision of Prof. Mikaela Iacobelli.

I obtained my Master's degree in mathematics from the University of Neuchâtel in 2020 and my Bachelor's degree in 2018, including one year as an Erasmus student at Durham University. My CV is available here.

Research

My research focuses on kinetic theory, in particular on Vlasov-type equations, which describe the dynamics of particles in plasmas. I am especially interested in the long-time behaviour and stability of such systems, including Landau damping.

More recently my work has concentrated on the quasineutral limit in plasmas for the Vlasov–Maxwell system, and on the global well-posedness of the magnetized Vlasov–Poisson equation.
